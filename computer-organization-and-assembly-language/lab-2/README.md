# 🖥️ COAAL - Lab 2

**Course:** Computer Organization & Assembly Language

---

## 🎯 Objectives

By completing this lab, students will be able to:

* Understand the importance of learning assembly language.
* Recognize the structure of registers and their subdivisions.
* Analyze how arithmetic instructions affect CPU flags.

---

## 📝 Exercises

1. **Importance of Assembly 📘**

   * Explain why computer science students should learn assembly language.

2. **Register Structure 🔧**

   * Show the **ECX register** and explain the position and size of `CX`, `CH`, and `CL`.

3. **Arithmetic & Flags ⚡**

   * Execute `ADD` instructions on given values of **EAX**.
   * Record the results of EAX and the status of flags (`CF`, `OF`, `SF`, `PF`, `AF`, `ZF`).

---

## 📂 Key Concepts

* Benefits of learning assembly programming.
* 32-bit registers and their subdivisions.
* Effects of arithmetic operations on flags.

---

> **Note:** This lab was **theoretical only**. No `.asm` (assembly) files are included in this directory.
